My Interests

I have a passion for sports, traveling, music, TV series and films. In my free time, I like to learn new things and expand my knowledge.

Sports

I enjoy playing football and basketball in my free time. I also like to watch sports events on TV.

I'm a huge fan of Inter Milan, I watch every match and, when I can, I really enjoy going to the stadium to support my team. I also like to play football with my friends and participate in local tournaments.

Traveling

I love traveling and exploring new places. I enjoy learning about different cultures and trying new foods.

I have visited almost all of Italy and I have also traveled to a lot of European countries, such as France, Spain, UK and many more. My favorite place I have visited is London as I really enjoyed the city and the culture. I also like to travel with my friends and family, as it is a great way to create memories and strengthen relationships.

Music

I have a great passion for music. I like to listen to different genres and sometimes play the guitar.

My favorite music genre is rap, I enjoy listening to artists like Travis Scott, Drake, Kendrick Lamar and Tupac. I also like to discover new artists from all over the world and attend live concerts whenever I can. Music is a big part of my everyday life as I like listening to it in every moment.

TV Series and films

I enjoy watching TV series and films, especially those with complex storylines and interesting characters.

My favorite TV series is "Breaking Bad", I really enjoyed the story and the character development. I also like to watch films from different genres, such as action, comedy, drama and science fiction. I enjoy going to the cinema with my friends and family to watch the latest releases.
